Key Rules
Camping: min 100 ft from water, 300 ft from trail corridor
Campfires: prohibited at all times in Desolation Wilderness
Bear canister required in Desolation
Dogs allowed on leash
Zone 33 (Lake Aloha): no camping
CA Campfire Permit required for stove use (free) — readyforwildfire.org
Barker Pass → Tahoe City: no permit required, dispersed camping within 300 ft of trail
Richardson Lake is outside Desolation — campfires allowed with free CA Campfire Permit
Shuttle Logistics
One-way trip — start at Echo Lake, finish at Tahoe City. Options: leave a car at the 64 Acres Park / Hwy 89 lot in Tahoe City before the trip; use WannaRideTahoe.com for a paid shuttle from Barker Pass or Echo Lake; or use Uber (operates in both Tahoe City and South Lake Tahoe).

Meetup Notes
Friend A: Meet at Echo Lake Chalet parking lot morning of June 11. Water taxi available across Echo Lakes (saves ~1.5 miles — highly recommended). Friend A exits at Barker Pass Trailhead end of Day 4 (June 14 afternoon).

Friend B: Meet at Barker Pass Trailhead end of Day 4 (June 14 evening). Camp ~0.75 miles in from the trailhead first night together. Barker Pass Rd is off Hwy 89 at Kaspian Campground — follow Blackwood Canyon Rd 7.5 miles to trailhead.
